Privacy Notice — Apex Convoy

Last updated: [DATE]

Apex Convoy is a live location-sharing app for motorcycle and car ride groups. This page explains, in plain language, what we collect, why, and who can see it.

What we collect

  • Account info: username, email address, and a password (stored securely, never shown to anyone).
  • Emergency details (optional but recommended): blood group and a contact phone number.
  • Vehicle details (optional): nickname, type, make, model, colour, and plate number.
  • Live location: only while you are actively in a ride — never in the background, and never when you're not using the app.
  • Ride history: distance, duration, and top speed for rides you complete, saved so you can look back on them.

Why we collect it

  • Your username, blood group, contact number, and vehicle details are shown to other riders in the same ride — this is the point of the app, and matters most if you ever need to send an SOS and someone needs to identify or find you quickly.
  • Your live location is shared with your ride group so everyone can see where each other are on the map, and is used to calculate your own distance, speed, and ride summaries.
We never sell your data, and we never share it with anyone outside the ride you're currently in.

Who can see what

  • Other riders in a ride you join can see your username, live location, blood group, phone number, and vehicle details for as long as you're in that ride together.
  • Admins of this app can see ride records (who hosted, who joined, when) to keep the service running, but do not see your password.
  • Nobody else. We don't share data with advertisers or other third parties.

How long we keep it

  • Your live location is only ever shared while you're in an active ride, and is not stored once the ride ends.
  • Your profile, garage, and ride history stay saved until you delete them.

Your choices

  • Blood group, phone number, and vehicle details are optional — you can leave them blank, though they're there to help someone identify or assist you in an emergency.
  • You can edit your profile and remove vehicles at any time from within the app.
  • You can permanently delete your account and all associated data from the "Delete profile" option in the app. This cannot be undone.
